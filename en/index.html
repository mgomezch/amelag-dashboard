About the Data Source & Unofficial Project

The measurements visualized here originate from the public open data of the AMELAG project (Wastewater Surveillance for Epidemiological Situation Assessment), a joint initiative by the Robert Koch Institute (RKI) and the German Environment Agency (UBA). Important: This website is an entirely private, unverified hobby project by Manuel Gómez and has no affiliation, endorsement, or connection with the Robert Koch Institute or the German Environment Agency. Visualisations are provided as-is without any warranty and do not represent official statements or assessments.

Why Linear and Logarithmic Visualization?

  • Linear Scale: Shows the actual physical concentration of viruses in wastewater, illustrating the absolute scale of major infection waves compared to low baseline periods.
  • Logarithmic Scale (Log₁₀): Because viruses replicate exponentially during the early phase of an outbreak, exponential growth appears as a straight line with constant slope. This facilitates early trend detection and enables simultaneous comparison of pathogens with widely differing baselines (e.g. SARS-CoV-2 vs. RSV).

Normalization and GAM Modeling

To compensate for variations caused by precipitation, snowmelt, or industrial inflows, measured viral loads are normalized by plant wastewater flow. A Generalized Additive Model (GAM) with adaptive spline smoothing is then applied to extract the underlying epidemiological trend with high resilience against individual measurement noise.
